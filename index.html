04 / BEFORE WE START

Before we
work together.

Which platforms do you work with?

I work with Meta Ads, including Facebook and Instagram, and Google Ads. I also handle social media strategy and on-page and off-page SEO.

How do you measure campaign success?

Success depends on the campaign goal. For lead generation, I review leads and lead quality. For sales campaigns, I review revenue, acquisition costs and return on ad spend. Tracking and reporting are agreed around your business goals.

Can we discuss a campaign for the USA, UK or Pakistan?

Yes. We can discuss your target market, audience, offer and campaign requirements. The published results above relate to Pakistan recruitment campaigns.

What do you need before starting?

Your business goals, target audience, current offer, website or landing page, previous campaign context and a clear budget. From there, we can define the scope and next steps.
