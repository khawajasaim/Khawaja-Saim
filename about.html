Working principles

How I think about growth.

Data before decisions

Campaign and channel decisions should be based on what performance data is showing, not attachment to a platform or tactic.

Business outcomes over vanity metrics

Reach and engagement matter when they support a wider goal. Lead quality, acquisition efficiency, revenue and customer movement matter more.

Creative is part of performance

Audience and media buying cannot compensate forever for weak hooks, messages or formats. Creative testing belongs inside the optimization loop.

Automation needs a good process

Automation should remove repetitive work and improve response speed while keeping the customer journey understandable and human.
