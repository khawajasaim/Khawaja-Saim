Case 01 · The Hiring

Meta lead generation

Challenge: generate qualified IT candidates at a lower acquisition cost than traditional recruitment channels.

57x

ROAS from PKR 9K ad spend to PKR 520K attributed revenue.

Strategy

Targeted IT audience segments and lookalike audiences.

Execution

Meta Instant Forms plus ad copy and visual testing.

Result

63 leads and PKR 520K attributed revenue.

Case 03 · Trots Tutor

UK app acquisition

Challenge: Meta activity was consuming budget without enough meaningful app installs.

90

App installs after acquisition emphasis shifted toward Google App Campaigns.

Strategy

Review channel performance and choose the stronger acquisition path.

Execution

Shift acquisition toward Google App Campaigns and optimize toward install volume.

Result

$0.36 avg CPC · 1.86% CTR · 26,506 impressions.
