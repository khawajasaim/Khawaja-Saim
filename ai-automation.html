Clear answers

Automation FAQ.

Is automation a replacement for marketing strategy?

No. Automation makes a good process faster and more consistent. It cannot fix a weak offer, poor targeting or unclear customer journey.

What can an AI receptionist do?

A well-designed system can answer common questions, collect caller details, check availability, support booking and trigger follow-up workflows.

Do you work only with one automation platform?

No. My positioning is based on the business workflow and outcome. Tools are selected according to the job rather than treated as the product itself.
